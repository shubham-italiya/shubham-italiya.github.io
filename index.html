Cash Flow Forecasting Model

Rebuilt 20262025; rebuilt Oct 2026
  • Built a pipeline that forecasts the next 30 days of daily cash inflow for a UK online retailer, from about one million real transactions cleaned into a 739-day net sales series.
  • Ran ADF stationarity tests on raw, log and differenced series and used them to set up a weekly seasonal ARIMA (SARIMA) model.
  • Compared a seasonal-naive baseline, SARIMA and a TensorFlow LSTM in a 6-fold rolling backtest; the LSTM had the lowest daily MAE (9% below SARIMA, 2% below the baseline), while SARIMA was best on 30-day totals.

Customer Payment Behaviour Classifier

University group project, rebuilt 2026Oct 2024 - Dec 2024; rebuilt Oct 2026
  • Built a model that flags customers likely to miss their next payment from six months of statement and payment history for 30,000 customers, reaching F1 0.54 and recall 0.57 on a held-out test set (34% higher F1 than a logistic-regression baseline).
